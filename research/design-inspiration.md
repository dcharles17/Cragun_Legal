# Design Inspiration - Top Law Firm Websites

## Color Scheme Selection
**Chosen Palette:** Navy Blue + Gray + Gold Accent
- **Primary (Navy):** #1a3a52 - Trust, stability, professionalism
- **Secondary (Gray):** #4a5568 - Modern, sophisticated
- **Accent (Gold):** #c9a961 - Premium, prestigious
- **White:** #ffffff - Clean, open
- **Dark Text:** #2d3748

## Key Design Elements from Top Performers

### 1. Stevens & Gailey (Ogden Competitor)
- Sticky header with phone always visible
- "Get Started" CTA buttons prominently placed
- Clean hierarchical navigation
- Bilingual services highlighted
- Trust indicators (years of experience)

### 2. Modern Design Best Practices
- **Mobile-First:** 60%+ traffic from mobile
- **Fast Loading:** Under 3 seconds
- **Clear Hierarchy:** Easy navigation to practice areas
- **Trust Building:** Reviews, credentials, experience
- **Strong CTAs:** Multiple contact points

### 3. Layout Structure
```
Header:
- Logo (left)
- Navigation menu (center/right)
- Phone number (prominent)
- "Free Consultation" CTA button

Hero Section:
- Compelling headline
- Service area mention
- Primary CTA
- Professional image

Practice Areas:
- Card-based layout
- Icons for each practice area
- Brief descriptions
- Links to detailed pages

Trust Section:
- Years of experience
- Service areas
- Client testimonials
- Credentials

Footer:
- Contact information
- Quick links
- Office locations
- Social media
```

## Typography
- **Headings:** Merriweather (serif) - professional, authoritative
- **Body:** Open Sans (sans-serif) - clean, readable
- **Minimum:** 16px body text for readability
