About Jake B. Cragun

Dedicated legal advocate for families in Northern Utah

Experienced Legal Representation You Can Trust

Jake B. Cragun is the founder of Cragun Legal, a law firm dedicated to providing personalized legal services to families and individuals throughout Weber County, Morgan County, and Davis County, Utah.

Commitment to Clients

At Cragun Legal, we understand that legal matters often arise during challenging times in people's lives. Whether you're navigating a divorce, planning for your family's future, or building a business, you need an attorney who listens, understands your unique situation, and provides practical solutions tailored to your needs.

Jake takes a client-focused approach to every case, ensuring you receive personalized attention and clear communication throughout the legal process. You're not just a case number—you're a person with real concerns, and your case deserves dedicated, thoughtful representation.

Practice Areas

Jake concentrates his practice in the following areas:

  • Family Law: Divorce, child custody, adoption, modifications, child support, and general family law matters
  • Estate Planning: Wills, trusts, powers of attorney, health care directives, and HIPAA releases
  • Business Development: Entity formation, contracts, and business counseling
  • Intellectual Property: Trademark and copyright protection

Community Connection

As a member of the Northern Utah community, Jake understands the values and needs of local families. With offices conveniently located in both Ogden and Morgan, Cragun Legal is accessible to clients throughout the region.

Jake is committed to making quality legal services available to those who need them, offering free initial consultations to discuss your legal concerns and explore your options.
